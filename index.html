About

Engineer at the intersection of robotics, controls, and validation.

Vedanshee has built and tested systems spanning autonomous mobile robots, pneumatic soft robotic actuation, embedded communication benches, HiL validation tooling, and industrial field robotics. She brings a practical systems mindset: understand the physics, instrument the hardware, validate the control behavior, and close the loop with data.

Her background combines graduate robotics training at USC, university-level recognition in mechanical engineering, internship experience across robotics and electronic systems testing, and current system-level V&V work on safety-critical electromechanical platforms.
